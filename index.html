Hello, I'm Diana

Policy is about people.

My work sits at the intersection of evidence, public policy and implementation. I care about what happens after a good idea is proposed: who helps shape it, what it takes to make it work, and whether it reaches the people it is meant to serve.

From Peru's public sector to applied research and graduate study at UC Berkeley, I've learned to approach development challenges with curiosity, collaboration and a commitment to public service.

CHAPTER 01 / PERU

Where my story begins.

My story begins in Peru. My interest in public service grew from a belief that people's lived experiences should matter in the decisions that shape their lives.

That conviction continues to guide the questions I ask and the kind of work I hope to contribute to.

Public serviceAdd your photograph: images/public-service.jpg
CHAPTER 02 / GOVERNMENT

Learning from public institutions.

I have worked with Peru's Ministries of Education, Women and Vulnerable Populations, and Foreign Trade and Tourism.

These experiences strengthened my understanding of how public institutions, implementation realities and collaboration shape policy decisions.

Evidence in practiceAdd your photograph: images/ipa.jpg
CHAPTER 03 / APPLIED RESEARCH

Bringing evidence closer to decisions.

As a Senior Policy Associate at Innovations for Poverty Action (IPA) Peru, I worked at the intersection of research, policy engagement and scaling.

That experience deepened my interest in what it takes to adapt promising approaches to local contexts and turn evidence into practical action.

UC BerkeleyAdd your photograph: images/berkeley.jpg
CHAPTER 04 / TODAY

Continuing to learn and build.

At the University of California, Berkeley, I am pursuing a Master of Development Engineering with a concentration in International Development Policy.

My current interests include evidence-informed policymaking, implementation, scaling and approaches that put communities at the center of development practice.
